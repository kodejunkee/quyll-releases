## 🆕 What's New in v0.9.3

<div align="center">

### *Quyll Speaks Your Language* 🌍

</div>

### 🌍 Full Multi-Language Interface
Quyll's interface is now fully localized in **five languages**: English, Español, Français, Português, and Deutsch.

- **Automatic detection.** On first launch, Quyll reads your operating system's language and switches to it if it's supported. Regional variants such as `es-MX`, `pt-BR`, or `fr-CA` map to the matching language. Unsupported locales fall back to English.
- **Choose it yourself.** A new **Language** tab in **Settings** lets you pick any supported language. Each one is listed in its own script, so you can find yours no matter which language the app is currently showing.
- **Go back to automatic anytime.** Pick **System Default** to clear your choice and follow your OS language again.
- **Covers the whole app.** More than 4,500 lines of translated strings span the Writing Studio, every World Database module, the Moodboard, Map Forge, the Relationship Graph, Outlines, and every settings, backup, sharing, and update screen.

> [!NOTE]
> **Your writing is never translated or changed.** Localization applies only to the app's interface. Your chapters, character sheets, lore, and other project content stay exactly as you wrote them.

### 🔄 Smoother In-App Updates
- **Restart straight from the app.** Once an update is installed, Quyll can **relaunch itself** to finish it.
- **Remembers pending restarts.** If an update has been installed but you haven't restarted yet, the Updates page shows that clearly instead of offering the same update again.
- **Clearer error messages in your language.** Connection drops, timeouts, rate limits, and server errors now show plain, translated explanations.

### 🛠️ Improvements & Polish
- **Settings redesign:** The Global Settings modal is reorganized around the new Language tab, with tabs for Account, Devices, Language, Appearance, Editor, Privacy, and About.
- **Moodboard & Map Forge:** Toolbars, inspectors, modals, and cards are fully localized, with context-aware labels.
- **Consistent dialogs:** Dialogs, dropdowns, toasts, error screens, loading states, and the sync indicator use the same terminology across every language.

## 🪶 What is Quyll?

Writing a novel or building a big story world usually means juggling half a dozen disconnected tools: one app for drafting chapters, another for character sheets, a spreadsheet for timelines, a whiteboard for visual inspiration, and notes scattered everywhere else.

**Quyll brings your whole creative universe under one roof.**

It combines a focused, distraction-free writing studio with a deep worldbuilding bible, interactive relationship graphs, visual moodboards, fantasy map generation, and structured plot outlines. Everything you create links directly to your manuscript as you type.

Quyll is **100% private and offline-first**. Your manuscripts and world lore stay on your computer in a local database. There are no forced subscriptions, no cloud lock-in, and **your creative work is never used to train AI.**

**Quyll is built for:**
- 📖 Novelists and fiction writers
- 🐉 Fantasy and sci-fi worldbuilders
- 🎬 Screenwriters and game narrative designers
- 🌙 Anyone who prefers calm, local-first software over cloud-only tools

## ✨ Features

### 🖋️ The Writing Studio
- **Distraction-free drafting:** A clean, typography-focused editor built for long sessions, with adjustable line spacing, paragraph spacing, page sizes, and margins.
- **Rich formatting:** Headings, block quotes, bullet and numbered lists, alignment (including justify), highlights, and inline images.
- **Chapter organization:** Reorder chapters with drag and drop, and track word counts, character counts, paragraphs, and estimated reading time live.
- **Interactive manuscript highlighting:** Character, location, and lore names light up as you write. Click any of them to open the full profile without losing your place.
- **Wikilinks & quick create:** Link to world entities inline, or create a new character or location straight from the editor.
- **Pinned reference bubbles:** Keep key character details or magic rules floating beside your draft while you write tricky scenes.
- **Offline grammar & style checking:** The private **Harper** engine flags grammar issues, passive voice, clichés, weak verbs, and pacing problems, and includes a built-in thesaurus. Nothing leaves your machine.
- **Find & replace**, a right-click formatting menu, and keyboard shortcuts for everything.
- **Session goals & progress:** Word goals, streaks, and pacing analytics on your project dashboard.

### 🛡️ Draft Safety
- **Autosave & crash recovery:** Your work is saved continuously. If something goes wrong, Quyll offers to restore your latest draft.
- **Empty-cut protection:** Accidentally selecting all and deleting can never overwrite a saved chapter.
- **Save History & checkpoints:** Browse previous saves and compare them word by word before restoring.

### 🌍 World Database: Your Living Story Bible
Every detail of your world stays organized, cross-referenced, and one click away:

| Module | What it tracks |
| :--- | :--- |
| 👤 **Characters** | Appearance, age, birthdays, personality, motivations, fears, backstory, abilities, status (Alive / Dead / Unknown), portraits, and custom attribute templates |
| 📍 **Locations** | Kingdoms, cities, ruins, and biomes, with climate, architecture, and cultural history |
| 🏰 **Organizations** | Guilds, courts, orders, secret societies, leadership hierarchies, and rival agendas |
| 🧬 **Species & Races** | Lifespans, traditions, languages, and dispositions |
| ⚔️ **Items & Artefacts** | Legendary weapons, cursed relics, and heirlooms, with their wielders and origins |
| ✨ **World Systems** | Magic, technology, and energy rules, including costs and limits, to keep your world consistent |
| 📜 **Lore** | Wars, myths, prophecies, pantheons, and historical timelines |
| 📖 **Glossary** | Custom terminology, conlang dictionaries, and pronunciation guides, matched live in your manuscript |

### 🕸️ Dynamic Character Relationship Graph
- **Map your cast** on an infinite, draggable canvas of character cards.
- **Color-coded bonds:** Family, Allied, Hostile, Romance, or custom ties, each with its own label.
- **Filter & focus:** Search your cast, filter by bond type, and jump from any character profile straight into the graph with that character centered.

### 🎨 Visual Moodboards
- **Infinite, zoomable canvas** for concept art, scenery, and color palettes.
- **Linked story elements:** Pin characters (showing their portrait if they have one), locations, artefacts, outlines, and other entities directly onto the board.
- **Notes, shapes, text & sketches:** Sticky notes, freehand drawing, styled shapes, and curved connector lines for storyboarding.
- **Multiple boards** per project, each with its own switcher.

### 🗺️ Map Forge
- **World maps:** Generate continents, archipelagos, and coastlines from a seed, then adjust relief and sea level.
- **Cities & settlements:** Start from editable walled-citadel or harbor-city layouts and define districts.
- **Dungeons & interiors:** Generate crypts, floorplans, and cavern complexes, and add notes to individual rooms.
- **Studio mode:** A freeform drawing canvas with a full tool dock and inspector.
- **Atlas library:** Nest maps inside each other (world → city → dungeon) and move between them with breadcrumbs.
- **Lore-linked pins:** Settlement, landmark, and battle markers connect directly to entries in your World Database.

### 📋 Outlines & Plot Planning
- **Act sequencing & beat cards** for scenes, events, and lore milestones.
- **Classic or custom structures:** Three-Act, the Hero's Journey, or your own.
- **Slide-out inspector** with scene synopses and target word counts.

### 🤝 Collaboration *(Pro)*
- **Invite co-authors, editors, and beta readers** as Owner, Editor, or Reviewer.
- **Chapter locking:** Real-time locks stop two people from editing the same scene at once.
- **Inline comments:** Leave review threads anchored to specific passages.

### 📤 Export & Portability
- **DOCX** for agents, editors, and critique partners.
- **Print-ready PDF** for proofreading.
- **Portable `.quyll` project archives** that hold your whole project and work across devices.

### 🎭 Themes & Personalization
- **Core themes:** Dark, Light, Oceans Blue, and Midnight, with 8 accent colors.
- **Pro genre themes:** Medieval, High Fantasy, Vintage Typewriter, and Manuscript Paper, with ambient effects.

## 🔒 Privacy & Your Data

- **🏠 Local-first by design.** Projects live in a local SQLite database on your computer. Every feature works without an internet connection.
- **🚫 No AI training, no scraping.** Quyll never reads, analyzes, or trains machine-learning models on your writing.
- **📦 You own your files.** Back up your `.quyll` projects to a USB drive, email them to yourself, or keep them in any cloud storage you already use.
- **☁️ Cloud is opt-in.** Sync and cloud backups only happen if you turn on Quyll Pro.

Read the full **[Privacy Policy](https://quyll.app/privacy.html)** and **[Terms of Service](https://quyll.app/terms.html)**.

## 📥 Download & Install

Get the newest installers from the **[Latest Release](https://github.com/kodejunkee/quyll-releases/releases/latest)** page (look under **Assets**) or from **[quyll.app](https://quyll.app)**.

| Platform | File | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 🪟 **Windows** (64-bit) | `Quyll_<version>_x64-setup.exe` | **Recommended** for most users |
| 🪟 **Windows** (64-bit) | `Quyll_<version>_x64_en-US.msi` | For managed / enterprise installs |
| 🍎 **macOS** (Universal) | `Quyll_<version>_universal.dmg` | Native on Apple Silicon & Intel · Signed |
| 🐧 **Linux** (64-bit) | `Quyll_<version>_amd64.AppImage` | Portable, runs on most distributions |
| 🐧 **Linux** (64-bit) | `Quyll_<version>_amd64.deb` | Debian / Ubuntu and derivatives |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Files ending in `.sig` and `latest.json` are used by Quyll's built-in updater. **You don't need to download them.**

<details>
<summary><b>🪟 Installing on Windows</b></summary>

1. Download `Quyll_<version>_x64-setup.exe`.
2. Double-click it and follow the installer.
3. Open Quyll from the Start menu or the desktop shortcut.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>🍎 Installing on macOS</b></summary>

1. Download `Quyll_<version>_universal.dmg`.
2. Open it and drag **Quyll** into your **Applications** folder.
3. Launch Quyll from Applications or Launchpad.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>🐧 Installing on Linux</b></summary>

**AppImage**
```bash
chmod +x Quyll_<version>_amd64.AppImage
./Quyll_<version>_amd64.AppImage
```

**Debian / Ubuntu (.deb)**
```bash
sudo apt install ./Quyll_<version>_amd64.deb
```

</details>

## 🚀 Quick Start

1. **Download and launch** Quyll.
2. **Create a new project**, or click **"Seed Sample Project"** on the home screen to explore a fully populated fantasy world with chapters, characters, lore, and moodboards already set up.
3. **Start writing.** As you add characters and places to the World Database, their names light up in your manuscript automatically.
4. **Explore the tools** in the sidebar: Moodboard, Relationship Graph, Map Forge, and Outlines.
5. **Make it yours** in **Settings** with themes, accent colors, editor spacing, and your preferred language.

## 🔄 Updating Quyll

Quyll updates itself. When a new version is available you'll be notified automatically, or you can open **Updates** in the sidebar at any time to:

- Browse the full release history

Your projects are never touched during an update.

## ❓ FAQ

<details>
<summary><b>Is Quyll really free?</b></summary>

<br>Yes. The desktop studio is free, with no trials, word limits, or locked creative tools. Quyll Pro is optional and only adds cloud sync, cloud backups, collaboration, and genre themes.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Do I need an account or internet connection?</b></summary>

<br>No. Quyll works fully offline with no account. You only need to sign in for optional Pro features.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Where are my projects stored?</b></summary>

<br>On your computer, in a local SQLite database. You can export any project as a portable `.quyll` archive at any time.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>What happens if I cancel Quyll Pro?</b></summary>

<br>You keep every word. Your projects stay on your computer with full access. Only cloud sync and automated cloud backups stop.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Can I export my manuscript?</b></summary>

<br>Yes. Export to **DOCX** for editors and agents, **PDF** for proofreading, or a full `.quyll` archive for backups and moving between devices.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Does Quyll use AI on my writing?</b></summary>

<br>No. Your manuscripts and notes are never read, analyzed, or used to train AI. Grammar checking runs entirely on your device.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Why is the source code not here?</b></summary>

<br>This repository is the **official public home for Quyll's releases**: installers, release notes, and issue tracking. Quyll's source code is developed privately.
</details>
